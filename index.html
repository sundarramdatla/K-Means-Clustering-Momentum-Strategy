Data and methodology

Reproducible interpretation of the calculations shown above
01 · Market universeCurrent S&P 500 constituents from Wikipedia, with Yahoo Finance adjusted prices. Monthly eligibility uses the 150 highest rolling average dollar-volume ranks, requiring 12 observations in a 60-month window.
02 · FeaturesRSI (20), Bollinger Bands (20), ATR (14), MACD, Garman–Klass volatility, volume and 1/2/3/6/9/12-month returns. Rolling Fama–French five-factor betas complement price features.
03 · Portfolio strategyFour K-Means clusters are initialized around RSI levels 30, 45, 55, and 70. Stocks assigned to cluster 3 are selected for next-month portfolios, with historical-price mean–variance / maximum-Sharpe weighting and equal-weight fallback.
04 · Performance and risk1,255 daily return observations, November 1, 2021–October 30, 2026. Sharpe = mean daily return / daily standard deviation × √252, assuming 0% risk-free rate. Drawdown is equity divided by its running peak minus one.

Important limitations: historical returns are in-sample, source universe selection may exhibit survivorship bias, trading costs and slippage are not included, and portfolio implementation assumptions affect realized outcomes. Data ends October 30, 2026, the last date in the supplied backtest; no later returns are inferred.
